# AI-Enhanced Phishing Detection for Small and Medium-Sized Businesses

**Subject Area:** AI Security · Threat Modeling · Natural Language Processing · Cybersecurity Research

---

## What It Does

This research investigates how large language models (LLMs) can be weaponized to generate highly convincing, context-aware phishing emails at scale — and explores a corresponding detection framework designed to identify AI-generated content in phishing campaigns. The paper proposes a dual-layer defense model combining linguistic pattern analysis with behavioral indicators to help SMBs detect and respond to next-generation phishing threats without enterprise-scale security budgets.

## Problem Addressed

Traditional phishing detection relies heavily on keyword filters, known sender reputation, and signature-based rules. Generative AI breaks all three of these assumptions. An attacker using GPT-class models can produce grammatically flawless, contextually tailored phishing emails that bypass conventional filters — with negligible cost and at scale. This work models that threat, quantifies the detection gap, and proposes a lightweight mitigation strategy appropriate for resource-constrained environments.

## Practical Relevance

As AI tooling becomes more accessible, the threat surface for social engineering attacks is expanding rapidly. This research has direct relevance for:

- Security teams evaluating AI risk in their email threat landscape
- Vendors building next-generation anti-phishing tooling
- Risk and compliance professionals updating email security policies for the generative AI era
- SMBs seeking defensible, cost-proportionate controls
